\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Online Algorithms.} Our work builds on the online knapsack problem \cite{gollapudi2019online} and ski-rental literature. We extend these frameworks to the hybrid routing setting with multiple constraint types.

\textbf{Learning-Augmented Algorithms.} We leverage the concept of algorithms with predictions \cite{purohit2018improving}, specifically using quantile regression to handle value uncertainty.

\textbf{LLM Serving Systems.} Systems like vLLM \cite{kwon2023efficient} and SGLang \cite{zheng2023sglang} focus on optimizing the throughput of a single instance. Our work is complementary, optimizing the \textit{routing} layer across multiple heterogeneous instances.
