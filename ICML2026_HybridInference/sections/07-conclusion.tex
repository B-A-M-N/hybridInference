\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a unified framework for optimizing LLM inference costs in a hybrid pricing environment. By modeling both quota and concurrency constraints, and solving the online problem with a learning-augmented primal-dual approach, we achieve near-optimal cost savings. Our analysis reveals that while quota is often the dominant constraint for current workloads, concurrency management becomes critical for larger models. Future work includes extending this framework to hierarchical quotas and handling soft latency SLOs.
